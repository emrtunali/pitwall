      Overtaking difficulty

      Each circuit: how much a car lost per lap while running within 1 s of the car ahead (after removing tyres, fuel and driver pace with the race model; 90 % cluster-bootstrap interval) against the number of on-track passes. Not published anywhere else: both are derived from raw timing.

      True race pace by team

      Seconds per lap behind the fastest team on equal tyres and fuel (the model's pace term), race by race. Darker = closer to the front; last column is the season median.

      Tyre degradation by circuit

      Seconds lost per lap of tyre age, from the race-pace model (fuel and track evolution removed). Bars show 90 % bootstrap intervals.

      Pit-stop time loss by circuit

      Measured from in-lap + out-lap versus two modelled racing laps, green-flag stops only (median).
