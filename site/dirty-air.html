What is real here. The lap-time numbers and the curve are measured from 2026 timing data. They are the race-pace model's residual on laps run at that gap, compared with laps in clean air (more than 3 s), after removing tyres, fuel and driver pace. The airflow is an illustration of the wake (rear-wing tip vortices and a widening turbulent region), not a CFD simulation.
